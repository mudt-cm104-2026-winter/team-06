# Glossary

Words we use in Movie Ratings, short and simple.

## Movie words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Movie | A film. In our app, one movie can get many ratings. |
| Title | The name of the movie, for example "Inception". |
| Genre | The kind of movie, for example comedy, drama or horror. |
| Director | The person who leads the making of the movie. |
| Rating | How good someone found the movie, from 1 (bad) to 5 (great). |
| Review | A longer opinion about a movie, in words. Our app only keeps the rating, not a review. |

## App words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Entry | One row in our table: a title, a rating and a person. |
| Average | All ratings added together, divided by the number of ratings. |
| Sort | Put the entries in an order, for example the best rating first. |
